## WHY THIS STRUCTURE

One customer — Otieno Odhiambo — crosses two systems in a single journey:
he self-serves on the Aventa Marketplace up to the payment screen, then
calls Customer Care, and agent Kim finishes the job for him inside
Canvas BSS/CRM — seeing his exact cart, resolving a change, and closing
the order. The story beat is continuity of experience, narrated as one
integrated platform — no caveats or "behind the scenes" disclaimers on
screen or in VO.

   caption entirely — replaced with a positive framing line ("One

   script file for the identical voice prompt and settings.
