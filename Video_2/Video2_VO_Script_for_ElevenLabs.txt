================================================================
1. ACT 1 — HOOK  (target ~14s)
================================================================
A customer starts a purchase online. Then life happens — a question
comes up, and they pick up the phone instead. What happens next usually
means starting over: re-explaining who they are, what they picked, and
where they got to.

================================================================
2. ACT 2 — PROBLEM  (target ~12s)
================================================================
For the customer, that's frustrating. For the operator, it's a lost
sale and a longer call — because the agent who picks up has no idea
what's already sitting in that customer's basket.

================================================================
3. ACT 3 — SOLUTION INTRO  (target ~10s)
================================================================
This is the Marketplace and Canvas BSS's Customer 360 — built on the
same 6D data model, so the shopper's basket and the agent's view of that
customer tell the same story.

================================================================
4. ACT 4 — MECHANISM  (target ~16s)
================================================================
Every product, price, and customer profile is modelled once, aligned to
TM Forum's SID information framework — so whichever door a customer
walks through, self-service storefront or Customer 360 in the contact
centre, they're looking at the same record.

================================================================
4B. ACT 4B — LINKED OR NOT  (target ~17s)  [NEW — v4]
================================================================
Not every Marketplace shopper is an Operator customer. Some check out
as Marketplace-only, with nothing to link — and that's fine. But when a
profile is linked, like Otieno's, Marketplace and Canvas BSS are
reading the exact same Party record — the same customer, from both
sides.

================================================================
5. SEGMENT 1 — THE MARKETPLACE: OTIENO SHOPS  (target ~36s)
================================================================
Otieno Odhiambo signs into the Marketplace on his phone. Browsing
Digital Content, he tries a few things — adding a gaming pass, then
swapping it out — before settling on PlayForge Cloud Gaming and a
Travel eSIM for an upcoming trip, ten gigabytes, thirty days. Both go
into his basket. He moves to checkout, confirms his delivery details,
and sees his total — twenty-four dollars forty-nine, tax included.
Then, at the payment step, he pauses. He'd rather just talk it through
— so he calls Customer Care.

================================================================
6. SEGMENT 2 — CANVAS BSS: KIM TAKES THE CALL  (target ~52s)
================================================================
On the other end, agent Kim signs into Canvas BSS. She searches
Otieno's number after connected with Otieno on call and lands straight
on his Customer 360 — profile, account, active service, and AI Insights
already flagging his upgrade potential. One click into his Subscription
Cart, and she's looking at the exact same two items, the exact same
twenty-four forty-nine, that he left behind in the Marketplace. No
re-explaining required. Otieno asks her to swap out the eSIM for a
different validity — she removes it, confirms the change, and adds it
back — then, seeing his trip dates on file, suggests a streaming add-on
for the flight. He agrees. She checks out on his behalf. Order placed —
successfully, and worth more than the basket he almost abandoned.

================================================================
7. ACT 8 — BUSINESS OUTCOMES  (target ~14s)
================================================================
The result: no repeat-explaining, faster call resolution, a stalled
sale rescued instead of lost, and an upsell that AI Insights made
obvious — all from one consistent view of the customer.

================================================================
8. ACT 9 — CLOSING TAGLINE  (target ~10s)
================================================================
One customer. One record. Any channel they choose — the Marketplace and
Canvas BSS, working from the same page.
