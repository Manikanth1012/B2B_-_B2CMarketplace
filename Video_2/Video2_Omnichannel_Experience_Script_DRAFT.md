## WHY THIS STRUCTURE

Unlike Video 1 (four parallel personas), Video 2 follows ONE customer —
Otieno Odhiambo — through a single journey that crosses two systems: he
self-serves on the Aventa Marketplace up to the payment screen, then
picks up the phone, and a Customer Care agent (Kim) finishes the job for
him inside Canvas BSS/CRM — seeing his exact cart, resolving a change,
and closing the order. The story beat is continuity, not integration.

IMPORTANT DISCLAIMER (carried in-frame, base caption, through Segment 2):
there is no live system integration between the Marketplace and Canvas
BSS/CRM in this demo — the two systems share the same seeded demo data
(same customer, same products, same prices) so the *experience* of
continuity can be shown. Script and on-screen caption must not imply a
real-time API handoff exists today; word it as "shown here" / "a
consistent customer record" rather than "automatically synced."

Confirmed from the two supplied recordings + CRM transcript:
- Marketplace side (~141s): Otieno signs in, browses Digital Content,
  adds PlayForge Cloud Gaming ($9.99/mo) and Travel eSIM — 10GB, 30 days
  ($14.50 one-off) to his basket, goes to checkout (delivery details →
  order summary, $24.49 incl. 16% VAT), reaches the payment method step
  (M-Pesa / card / bank transfer / add-to-bill / instalments) — and the
  recording ends there, i.e. he stops short of paying.
- CRM side (~87s, per transcript): agent Kim logs into Canvas BSS,
  searches Otieno's MSISDN, lands on his Customer 360 (Profile > Account
  > Service), opens the Subscription Cart and sees the SAME two items and
  the SAME $24.49 total he left behind in the Marketplace. She removes
  the eSIM to change it (confirmation prompt: "this plan was added from
  another channel"), then re-adds it plus a third item — StreamNova
  Premium 4K ($12.99) — and checks out on his behalf. Order Placed
  Successfully. New total: $37.48 incl. VAT.
- That upsell is the pay-off beat: the agent doesn't just complete the
  stalled purchase, she adds value to it.
