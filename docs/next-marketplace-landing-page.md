# Requested: marketplace common landing page

Raised 29 Jul 2026, to start after the knowledge base work.

## What was asked

- A **common marketplace landing page for everyone** — the public front door.
- **Partners, Retail and Enterprise** named at the top for new onboarding / login,
  each routing into the persona console already built.
- The **existing four-persona login page stays**, for demo purposes.
- Take the existing pages and branding as the base and improvise: running
  carousels, promotional banners, and so on.
- Inspiration from publicly available marketplace UI/UX.

## Assets supplied

`D:\Claude\Projects\B2B_Marketplace\images` — **64 files, 46 MB**.

Named sets include `Ad_Banners.png`, `Coroussels.png`, `Device_Images_Collage1.png`,
`Mobile.png`, and ~60 `Professional_product_photograph_of_a_*` product shots
(IoT gateways, LoRa, PIR sensors, PTZ cameras, EV chargers, POS terminals, VR
headsets, racks, beacons, and more).

## Constraint to resolve before building

46 MB of PNGs cannot ship as-is — a landing page carrying even a fraction of that
would be unusable on anything but a fast connection. The product shots are also
far larger than their rendered size. Needs a decision on conversion to WebP,
resizing to display dimensions, and which images actually appear above the fold.
